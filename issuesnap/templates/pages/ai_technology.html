AI Technology

Real Computer Vision, Not Fake AI

Every prediction is a real model output. Every confidence score is genuine. No hardcoded values, ever.

Image Preprocessing (OpenCV)

Raw images are loaded with OpenCV, converted from BGR to RGB, resized to 224×224, and normalized to [0,1] before inference.

img = cv2.cvtColor(img, cv2.COLOR_BGR2RGB)
img = cv2.resize(img, (224, 224))
img = img.astype(np.float32) / 255.0

Model Architecture

MobileNetV2 or EfficientNetB0 with transfer learning. Final layers fine-tuned on civic issue datasets. Supports 7 categories.

  • Base: MobileNetV2 (ImageNet weights)
  • Added: GlobalAveragePooling → Dense(256) → Dropout(0.4) → Dense(7, softmax)
  • Input: 224×224×3
  • Output: 7-class softmax probabilities

NLP Text Analysis

TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity detect potential duplicate complaints. Keyword extraction identifies urgency and category.

  • Text cleaning and stopword removal
  • Keyword frequency scoring
  • Urgency keyword detection
  • Category suggestion from text
  • Cosine similarity for duplicates

Priority Prediction

Rule-based priority scoring considers category weight, urgency keywords, AI confidence, and description detail. Fully transparent reasoning.

Critical: Score ≥ 70 High: ≥ 50 Medium: ≥ 30 Low: < 30
