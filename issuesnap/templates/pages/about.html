About IssueSnap

AI-Powered Civic Issue Reporting

A final-year engineering research project that applies cutting-edge AI to solve real civic infrastructure problems.

Our Mission

Making Cities Smarter, One Issue at a Time

IssueSnap bridges the gap between citizens and municipal authorities using artificial intelligence. Citizens can report potholes, broken streetlights, garbage overflow, and water leakage using simple photo uploads, while our AI engine classifies issues automatically.

Every claim our system makes is backed by real model inference — no fake confidence scores, no hardcoded predictions. We built this platform with academic integrity and research rigor.
