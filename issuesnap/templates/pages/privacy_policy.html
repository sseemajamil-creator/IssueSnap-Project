Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect your name, email address, complaint text, uploaded images, and geographic coordinates when you submit a complaint. Location data is used only to map issues and calculate hotspots.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to process complaints, run AI analysis, detect duplicates, and notify you of status updates. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. AI Processing

Uploaded images are processed by our computer vision model for classification. Images are stored securely and not shared publicly without your consent.

4. Public Complaint Map

The public issue map shows complaint locations, category, and status. Your personal information (name, email, CNIC) is never shown publicly.

5. Data Security

Passwords are hashed using Django's PBKDF2 algorithm. All inputs are validated and sanitized. CSRF protection is enabled on all forms.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact us at our contact page.
